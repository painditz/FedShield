Byzantine Attack Resistance
Comparing model accuracy under a 20% Byzantine Sign-Flipping attack. Notice how Coordinate Median and Trimmed Mean neutralize the attack.

⚠️ Sign-Flipping Attack

Malicious clients invert their weight updates ($-\text{scaling} \cdot \Delta w$). Standard FedAvg blindly averages these, causing test accuracy to plummet to 11.4%.

🛡️ Coordinate Median

Calculates the coordinate-wise median across clients. Outlier inverted updates cannot shift the median coordinate, restoring test accuracy to 28.4%.

🛡️ Trimmed Mean

Discards the top and bottom $\beta$ fraction (e.g. 20%) of updates per coordinate before averaging, eliminating poisoned updates and restoring accuracy to 28.4%.
